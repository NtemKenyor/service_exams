CROSS RIVER STATE CIVIL SERVICE
DIRECTORS’ PROMOTION EXAMINATION
100-Question Test Bank for Progression to Permanent Secretary
SECTION A — CURRENT AFFAIRS AND GOVERNANCE (Questions 1–25)
1.1. Who is the current President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria?
Answer: Bola Ahmed Tinubu.
2.2. Who is the current Vice-President of Nigeria?
Answer: Kashim Shettima.
3.3. Who is the current Governor of Cross River State?
Answer: Senator Prince Bassey Edet Otu.
4.4. Who is the current Deputy Governor of Cross River State?
Answer: Dr. Peter A. Odey.
5.5. Who is the current Head of Service of Cross River State?
Answer: Mr. Orok Bassey Okon.
6.6. Who is the current Secretary to the Cross River State Government?
Answer: Prof. Anthony Owan-Enoh.
7.7. What is the stated development philosophy of the present Cross River State administration?
Answer: The People First philosophy/ideology.
8.8. What was the total outlay presented for Cross River State’s 2026 proposed budget in October 2025?
Answer: ₦780.59 billion.
9.9. What major development measurement shift did Governor Otu announce in presenting the 2026 budget?
Answer: Greater emphasis on Human Development Index (HDI), rather than GDP alone, as a measure of development impact.
10.10. What was the capital/recurrent split announced for the proposed 2026 Cross River budget?
Answer: ₦519.6 billion capital and ₦260.96 billion recurrent.
11.11. What major tourism/economic asset was approved for comprehensive remodelling and rehabilitation by the Cross River State Executive Council in May 2026?
Answer: Calabar International Convention Centre (CICC).
12.12. What renewable-energy installation was approved for the renovated Governor’s Lodge in Ogoja?
Answer: 850 KW hybrid solar panels with lithium storage.
13.13. What major institutional reform did the Cross River State Head of Service emphasise regarding promotion?
Answer: Promotion should be performance-based rather than based merely on length of service.
14.14. What mode did the Cross River State Head of Service say future promotional examinations would use?
Answer: Computer-based testing.
15.15. Why is a Director preparing for Permanent Secretary expected to understand current public policy?
Answer: Because a Permanent Secretary provides senior administrative leadership, policy advice, coordination and implementation support to government.
16.16. What is the purpose of a Medium-Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF)?
Answer: To link government policy priorities and fiscal projections with medium-term resource allocation and budgeting.
17.17. What is the difference between recurrent and capital expenditure?
Answer: Recurrent expenditure funds regular operations; capital expenditure funds assets/projects with development or investment value.
18.18. What is fiscal responsibility in public administration?
Answer: Responsible management of public resources within legal, fiscal and budgetary limits, with attention to sustainability, transparency and value for money.
19.19. What is the primary purpose of public accountability?
Answer: To ensure public officers explain and justify decisions, use of resources and performance to authorised oversight institutions and citizens.
20.20. Why is evidence-based policy important?
Answer: It improves the quality of decisions by grounding policy choices in reliable data, analysis, evidence and measurable outcomes.
21.21. What is the significance of the 2020/2021 Public Service Rules to senior civil servants?
Answer: They provide the governing framework for conduct, appointment, discipline, leave, promotion, retirement and other administrative matters in the public service.
22.22. What is one major current economic challenge facing Nigeria?
Answer: Cost-of-living pressure, including high food, transport and energy costs.
23.23. What recent federal policy direction seeks to encourage domestic oil refining and investment?
Answer: Measures to improve crude supply arrangements and investment conditions for domestic refining and deep-water projects.
24.24. What is the strategic importance of digital transformation in the civil service?
Answer: It improves efficiency, service delivery, records management, transparency, data use and access to government services.
25.25. Why should a Permanent Secretary understand the relationship between policy, budget and implementation?
Answer: Because policy requires resources and institutional execution; the budget translates priorities into funded programmes and implementation delivers outcomes.
SECTION B — PUBLIC SERVICE RULES, ETHICS AND ADMINISTRATIVE PRACTICE (Questions 26–60)
26.26. What is the principle of political neutrality in the civil service?
Answer: A career public servant should serve the government of the day professionally and impartially, without allowing partisan interests to compromise official duties.
27.27. What is official secrecy?
Answer: The obligation to protect confidential official information and disclose it only through authorised channels.
28.28. What is conflict of interest?
Answer: A situation in which a public officer’s private interest could improperly influence, or appear to influence, official judgement.
29.29. What should a senior officer do when faced with a potential conflict of interest?
Answer: Declare the conflict through the appropriate channel and, where required, recuse himself or herself from the affected decision.
30.30. What is due process in public administration?
Answer: Following the prescribed legal, procedural and administrative steps before taking an official decision.
31.31. Why is delegation important at Permanent Secretary level?
Answer: It enables efficient distribution of responsibilities while retaining appropriate supervision and accountability.
32.32. Does delegation automatically transfer ultimate accountability?
Answer: No. Delegation transfers authority for specified tasks; the delegating officer retains appropriate oversight and accountability under the applicable rules.
33.33. What is a minute in government administration?
Answer: A concise official record used to present facts, analysis, recommendations, decisions or requests on a file.
34.34. What is the difference between a minute and a memo?
Answer: A minute is commonly recorded on an official file for internal consideration and decision; a memo is a formal written communication used to convey information or seek action/approval.
35.35. What is an official file?
Answer: An organised record of correspondence, decisions, evidence and actions relating to a particular subject or case.
36.36. Why must government records be accurate?
Answer: Because they provide evidence of decisions, support continuity, accountability, audit and institutional memory.
37.37. What is insubordination?
Answer: A wilful refusal to obey a lawful and reasonable official instruction from an authorised superior.
38.38. What is misconduct?
Answer: Behaviour that breaches established laws, rules, regulations, ethical standards or official obligations.
39.39. What is gross misconduct?
Answer: Serious misconduct of a nature that may warrant severe disciplinary consequences under the applicable rules.
40.40. What is the purpose of disciplinary procedure?
Answer: To establish facts fairly, protect due process and determine an appropriate response to alleged misconduct.
41.41. Why should a disciplinary decision be evidence-based?
Answer: To ensure fairness, defensibility, consistency and compliance with due process.
42.42. What is merit-based promotion?
Answer: Advancement based on demonstrated competence, performance, qualifications and applicable criteria rather than patronage or length of service alone.
43.43. What is performance management?
Answer: A systematic process of setting expectations, monitoring performance, giving feedback and assessing results against agreed objectives.
44.44. What is the role of a Permanent Secretary in performance management?
Answer: To translate policy priorities into departmental objectives, ensure implementation, monitor results and hold relevant structures accountable.
45.45. What is the principle of value for money?
Answer: Obtaining the appropriate combination of economy, efficiency and effectiveness in the use of public resources.
46.46. What is procurement integrity?
Answer: Conducting procurement transparently, fairly and in accordance with applicable procurement laws and procedures.
47.47. Why should procurement decisions be documented?
Answer: To provide an auditable trail showing how decisions were reached and whether due process was followed.
48.48. What is administrative discretion?
Answer: The lawful authority to choose among permissible options when the law or rules leave room for judgement.
49.49. What is abuse of discretion?
Answer: Using discretionary power arbitrarily, for an improper purpose, in bad faith or outside lawful limits.
50.50. What is natural justice in administrative decision-making?
Answer: Fair hearing and impartial decision-making, including giving an affected person a reasonable opportunity to respond where applicable.
51.51. Why should a Permanent Secretary distinguish policy from administration?
Answer: Policy determines broad governmental direction; administration organises people, resources and processes to implement approved policy.
52.52. What is the principle of continuity in the civil service?
Answer: Institutions and administrative processes continue to function despite changes in political leadership.
53.53. What is institutional memory?
Answer: The knowledge and records accumulated by an organisation that enable continuity and informed decision-making.
54.54. What is an establishment register used for?
Answer: To maintain authorised information about posts, staffing structure and related establishment matters.
55.55. What is manpower planning?
Answer: Forecasting and aligning staffing needs, skills and deployment with organisational objectives.
56.56. Why is succession planning important for senior civil service leadership?
Answer: It reduces institutional vulnerability by developing capable officers to assume critical responsibilities when vacancies occur.
57.57. What is a circular?
Answer: An official communication issued to convey policy, instructions, procedures or information to a defined group of officers or MDAs.
58.58. What is an approval in official administration?
Answer: A formal authorisation by the competent authority to proceed with a proposed action.
59.59. Why should a Director not implement an action requiring higher approval before approval is obtained?
Answer: Doing so may breach delegated authority, create financial/administrative liability and undermine due process.
60.60. What is the central ethical obligation of a senior civil servant?
Answer: To serve the public interest with integrity, impartiality, competence, accountability and fidelity to the law.
SECTION C — CROSS RIVER STATE AFFAIRS AND DEVELOPMENT (Questions 61–80)
61.61. What is the capital of Cross River State?
Answer: Calabar.
62.62. How many Local Government Areas does Cross River State have?
Answer: 18.
63.63. Name the three senatorial districts of Cross River State.
Answer: Cross River North, Cross River Central and Cross River South.
64.64. Which major international tourism event is strongly associated with Calabar?
Answer: Calabar Carnival.
65.65. Why is tourism strategically important to Cross River State?
Answer: It can diversify state revenue, create jobs, attract investment and strengthen the hospitality and creative economy.
66.66. What is the strategic significance of Obudu Ranch Resort to Cross River State?
Answer: It is a major tourism and investment asset with potential for high-value tourism, employment and regional economic development.
67.67. What is the role of the Cross River State Civil Service Commission?
Answer: To exercise its statutory functions relating to recruitment, appointments, promotion, discipline and other personnel matters within the state civil service, subject to applicable law.
68.68. What is the role of the Head of Service?
Answer: To provide professional leadership and coordination for the state civil service and advise government on civil service administration and reforms.
69.69. What is the significance of the 2026 Cross River budget for public administration?
Answer: It provides the legal and fiscal framework for implementing government priorities and programmes during the fiscal year.
70.70. Why should MDAs align annual budgets with the State Development Plan?
Answer: To ensure public spending supports strategic priorities and avoids fragmented or ad hoc expenditure.
71.71. What did the 2026 budget call circular emphasise about ongoing projects?
Answer: They should be included in budget proposals, while non-performing activities should not simply be rolled over.
72.72. What should accompany a proposal for a new capital project under the cited budget guidance?
Answer: Required approval and, where applicable, bills of quantities and supporting justification.
73.73. Why should recurrent items not be disguised as capital expenditure?
Answer: Because it distorts the budget, weakens fiscal transparency and can undermine proper financial control.
74.74. What is the role of the Cross River State House of Assembly in the budget process?
Answer: It considers, scrutinises, amends where constitutionally permitted and appropriates public expenditure through legislation.
75.75. What is the relationship between the Governor and the State Executive Council?
Answer: The Governor leads the executive branch and the Executive Council supports collective consideration and implementation of governmental policy and decisions.
76.76. Why is Calabar’s convention infrastructure economically significant?
Answer: It can attract conferences, business events, tourism expenditure and private-sector activity.
77.77. What is the strategic value of renewable energy projects for rural communities?
Answer: They can improve reliable electricity access, support businesses and reduce dependence on costly conventional power sources.
78.78. Why should a Permanent Secretary understand Cross River’s agricultural potential?
Answer: Agriculture can support food security, value chains, employment, rural livelihoods and economic diversification.
79.79. What is the importance of MSMEs to Cross River’s development?
Answer: They create employment, broaden the productive base, stimulate local supply chains and support inclusive economic growth.
80.80. Give three areas a Cross River Permanent Secretary should consider when evaluating a development programme.
Answer: Alignment with state priorities; measurable outcomes/value for money; sustainability and institutional capacity.
SECTION D — SHORT MEMOS, LETTERS AND EXECUTIVE WRITING (Questions 81–100)
81.81. Draft a short memo to the Head of Service recommending a quarterly performance review of Directors.
Expected response: Model answer should contain: To/From/Date/Subject; brief background; evidence/problem; recommendation; implementation arrangements; conclusion and request for approval.
82.82. Draft a memo seeking approval to conduct a training programme for senior officers on digital transformation.
Expected response: Include justification, objectives, target officers, estimated cost/funding source, expected outcomes, implementation timeline and approval sought.
83.83. Draft a letter inviting a Permanent Secretary to a policy coordination meeting.
Expected response: Use formal government style; state purpose, date/time/venue, agenda or expected contribution and appropriate closing.
84.84. Draft a memo reporting delayed implementation of a government programme.
Expected response: State facts objectively; identify causes; quantify impact where possible; propose corrective actions; indicate risks and request decision.
85.85. Draft a letter acknowledging receipt of a petition against a public officer.
Expected response: Acknowledge receipt without prejudging the case; state that the matter will be handled in accordance with applicable procedures.
86.86. Draft a memo recommending an inter-ministerial committee to address a cross-cutting policy issue.
Expected response: Define the problem, mandate, membership principles, deliverables, reporting line, timeframe and resources.
87.87. Draft a letter requesting information from an MDA for preparation of the annual budget.
Expected response: Specify the information required, format, deadline, responsible officer and consequences of late submission.
88.88. Draft a memo recommending a review of an underperforming public project.
Expected response: Present evidence, causes, options, cost implications, risks and preferred recommendation.
89.89. Draft a brief official response to a media allegation concerning an MDA.
Expected response: Use verified facts, avoid speculation or defamatory language, identify the authorised communication channel and state any corrective action where appropriate.
90.90. Draft a memo seeking approval to digitise an MDA’s records.
Expected response: Cover business case, records inventory, security/privacy, technology, cost, implementation phases, change management and measurable benefits.
91.91. Write a 150-word briefing note explaining why merit-based promotion improves the civil service.
Expected response: A strong answer should connect merit to competence, productivity, fairness, institutional trust, accountability and service delivery.
92.92. Write a short policy note on improving punctuality and attendance in an MDA.
Expected response: Address diagnosis, clear expectations, monitoring, leadership example, lawful sanctions and positive incentives.
93.93. Draft a letter communicating an approved policy change to Heads of Department.
Expected response: Clearly state the decision, effective date, required actions, reporting arrangements and responsible authority.
94.94. Write a short memo advising against implementing an instruction that appears inconsistent with extant rules.
Expected response: Respectfully state the issue, cite the applicable authority/rule, identify risk and propose a lawful alternative.
95.95. Draft a memo requesting approval for an emergency response to a service-delivery disruption.
Expected response: State urgency, facts, immediate intervention, estimated cost, authority required, risk controls and reporting arrangements.
96.96. Write a short executive brief on the role of data in monitoring state development programmes.
Expected response: Explain baseline data, indicators, targets, verification, dashboards, evaluation and evidence-based decisions.
97.97. Draft a letter to an MDA requesting a corrective action plan after an audit finding.
Expected response: Reference the audit issue, request management response, specify corrective measures, responsible officers and deadline.
98.98. Draft a one-page memo proposing a leadership development programme for future Permanent Secretaries.
Expected response: Include competency framework, target group, modules, mentoring/coaching, assessment, duration and expected institutional benefits.
99.99. Draft a short memo on managing conflict between two senior officers.
Expected response: Emphasise facts, impartial hearing, applicable rules, mediation where appropriate, documentation and protection of service objectives.
100.100. Draft a one-page executive memo answering: “What would you do in your first 90 days as Permanent Secretary?”
Expected response: A strong response should cover stakeholder engagement, policy/mandate review, people and performance, budget/resource review, service-delivery priorities, risk management, quick wins, reporting and measurable outcomes.

Scoring Rubric for Questions 81–100
Excellent (80–100%): Accurate, concise, logically structured, evidence-led, rule-compliant, decision-oriented and professionally written.
Good (60–79%): Substantively sound with minor omissions or weaknesses in structure, analysis or expression.
Fair (40–59%): Demonstrates partial understanding but lacks depth, precision, evidence or clear recommendation.
Poor (0–39%): Materially inaccurate, poorly structured, non-responsive, procedurally unsafe or inadequately written.
